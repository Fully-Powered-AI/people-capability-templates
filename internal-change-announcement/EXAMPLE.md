# Internal change announcement: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The companies, the people, the system, the dates and the amounts below are all invented. Tallowmere Supply is an invented 130-person building-supplies distributor with two depots. In August it bought Fenbrook Fixings, an invented 12-person supplier with one branch. Tallowmere's people and operations coordinator prepares the announcement of one change: Fenbrook's branch moves onto Tallowmere's ordering system. First come the inputs the coordinator supplied, in full, then the pack the workflow template returned.

## The inputs

### 1. Company settings

```
Today: Monday 9 November 2026
Run by: People and operations coordinator
Working days: Monday to Friday
Closed: Thursday 26 and Friday 27 November 2026
Manager briefing, reminder and check-in: the defaults
Facts approved by: Dana Okafor, operations director
Wording approved by: People and operations coordinator
HR contact: HR manager
```

### 2. The change brief (typed by the coordinator after a call with Dana)

```
Change: From Monday 7 December 2026, the Fenbrook branch keeps its stock
  and places and receives stock orders in Brindlestock, Tallowmere's
  ordering system.
Why: Since Tallowmere bought Fenbrook in August, the two companies keep
  stock records in different places. Tallowmere's depots cannot see
  Fenbrook's stock and Fenbrook cannot see Tallowmere's, so customers
  are sometimes told an item is out of stock when the other company has
  it. One system lets both companies see all the stock.
What changes:
  - Fenbrook's order spreadsheet takes its last orders on Friday
    4 December.
  - Branch orders over $2,500 are approved in Brindlestock by Tallowmere's
    purchasing team, within one working day, instead of by the branch
    manager.
  - Tallowmere's depots see Fenbrook's stock in Brindlestock from
    7 December.
What stays the same: The branch team, its manager, the counter, opening
  hours and customer accounts stay as they are. Nobody's job, pay or
  hours change. Tallowmere purchasing already approves its own orders
  in Brindlestock the same way.
Decided by: Dana Okafor, operations director, Tallowmere.
Announcement: Tuesday 17 November 2026. Dana tells the branch in person
  at the Fenbrook branch at 8:00.
Support: Brindlestock training at the Fenbrook branch, Monday 23 November
  or Wednesday 25 November, 9:00 to 11:00. Everyone at the branch
  attends one. Marisol books people in.
Questions: Marisol Reyes for anything about the branch; Owen Pike for
  Brindlestock and approvals.
Managers keep it to themselves until Dana has told the branch.
Not for this announcement:
  - Gil Ashby, Fenbrook's founder, retires at the end of the year. HR is
    announcing that separately in December.
  - We are looking at whether to give up Fenbrook's second storage unit
    next year. Nothing is decided.
Dana's draft opening: "It has been decided that Fenbrook will move onto
  Brindlestock. It's just a small tweak to how orders get placed."
```

# Internal change announcement

One decided change, announced well: managers briefed first, a message for each group in the right order, talking points, and the questions people will ask, answered only from the decision or marked open. Every message is a draft that a person approves and sends.

*Capability template · People and HR · version 0.1.0 · [Capabilities Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/capabilities/internal-change-announcement/)*

## The outcome

People hear about a change in the right order, from the right person, and in words that match how much it affects them. Managers know before their teams do and have something to say when they are asked. The group whose daily work changes most hears first, in a meeting where people can ask questions; if no such meeting is planned, the pack says so before anything goes out.

Every message rests on the same numbered list of facts, so the version the warehouse hears matches the version the office reads. What stays the same is said as plainly as what changes. When people ask something the decision does not answer, the answer is not guessed in front of them: the question goes back to the person who decided, and a message says it is still open only once that person has confirmed it. Only questions the decision really leaves open are raised; nothing is invented to look thorough.

Some things never go into these messages. Anything about one person's job, pay, hours or terms of employment is held back and left to HR, and so is anything the brief marks as not for announcement.

## Who starts the work, and when

The internal communications lead, the people lead or the operations lead runs it once a change is decided and before anyone outside the decision has been told, ideally a week or more ahead. It runs again when a fact changes or an open question gets its answer.

The person who made the decision approves the facts. The lead approves the wording. Each sender reads and sends their own message.

## What they supply

- **The change brief:** what is changing and when, why, what stays the same, who decided, the day it will be announced, any meetings or training already planned, who answers questions, and anything that is not for this announcement.
- **The audience list:** each group affected, roughly how many people, what changes for them, how big a change it is for them, who tells them and how.
- **The company settings:** working days, closed days, how far ahead managers are briefed and when the reminder and check-in go out.

## What comes out

One announcement pack, ready for review:

- the facts, numbered;
- who hears first and when, dated in your working days;
- a draft message for each group and each moment, with the facts it uses;
- talking points for each person who tells a group or answers its questions;
- the questions people will ask, each answered from the facts or marked as needing an answer;
- what has been held back, and why;
- checks on wording, on the size given to each group, and on any group that needs a meeting it does not yet have.

Nothing is sent, posted or scheduled.

## What has to change for your company

Decide how far ahead managers hear and when the reminder and the check-in go out; the template starts from careful defaults. Write down what counts as a routine, noticeable or major change for your people. Name the channels you actually use, and who approves facts and wording. Name the HR contact who takes anything held back about a person's employment. Add your own words to the lists the drafts avoid. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the brief and the list you supply; it needs no connection to email, chat or your HR system.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the offboarding method in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes: a handover written by the departing person and reviewed by the manager, finished well before the final week; the topics a handover covers, including what the person knows that is written nowhere; a recorded removal for every system and account; returning and wiping devices, forwarding mail for an agreed period and changing shared accounts; separate steps for HR, IT and the manager, each with an owner and a date; telling the team at a time agreed with the departing person; flagging gaps for the manager instead of guessing; and leaving termination and redundancy letters to qualified people. Fully Powered AI narrowed the work to access, equipment and handover; built it from the company's own access register and equipment list, with stated column headers and a stop when one does not match; added the flags for a system or item named elsewhere but missing from a list, an account with no admin owner, admin rights, company data with no named receiver, and a handover with no receiver, no location or a date after the last day; kept the reason for leaving and every sign-in detail out of the checklist; and made every removal, transfer and message a step a named person takes after the HR lead approves. No third-party text is reused.

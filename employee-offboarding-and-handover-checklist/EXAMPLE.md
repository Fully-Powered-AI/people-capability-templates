# Employee offboarding and handover checklist: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the roles, the systems, the project, the equipment and the dates below are all invented. The company is an invented 80-person management consultancy. Its senior project coordinator in the delivery team has resigned. First come the inputs the HR lead and the head of delivery supplied, in full, then the checklist the workflow template returned.

## The inputs

### 1. Departure record (from the HR lead)

```
Role: Senior project coordinator, Delivery team
Manager: Head of delivery
Departure confirmed: Friday 6 November 2026
Last working day: Friday 4 December 2026
Not working before then: office closed Thursday 26 and Friday 27 November;
  booked leave Monday 30 November
Reason: moving to another firm
Today: Monday 9 November 2026
```

### 2. Access register (exported for the senior project coordinator)

```

Email and calendar,Own mailbox,Standard user,IT lead,No,Mailbox
Shared drive,Delivery folder,Editor,IT lead,No,Files
Project tracker,Own account,Admin,Head of delivery,No,None
Time and billing system,Own account,Approver,Finance manager,No,None
Client file-sharing site,Delivery team login,Editor,,Yes,None
Video meetings,Own account,Host,IT lead,No,Recordings
Travel booking,Own account,Booker,Office manager,No,None
```

### 3. Equipment list (exported for the senior project coordinator)

```

IT-0519,Mobile phone,2025-01-20,Carried
IT-0233,Monitor,2024-03-11,Office
FAC-118,Building pass,2023-09-04,Carried
```

### 4. Handover list (typed by the head of delivery)

```
1. Weekly client status reports for three accounts. Recurring, every
   Friday. Kept in the shared drive, Delivery/Status reports. Relied on by
   the three client leads. Receiver: Project coordinator.
2. Monthly resource plan. Recurring, first Monday of the month; next on
   7 December. Kept in the project tracker. Relied on by me and the
   finance manager. Receiver: not decided yet.
3. Harbour Road project close-out. Open, due 18 December. Kept in the
   project tracker and the shared drive. Relied on by the Harbour Road
   client lead and the client's facilities manager. Receiver: Senior
   consultant, by 11 December.
4. Supplier invoice approvals for Harbour Road. Open, until close-out.
   Relied on by the finance manager. Receiver: Senior consultant.
Mailbox and shared-drive files go to the Project coordinator.
```

### 5. Offboarding standard (set by the HR lead)

```
Working days: Monday to Friday
Handover document due: 10 working days before the last working day
Handover complete: 3 working days before the last working day
Access removed: 17:30 on the last working day
Mail: out-of-office and forwarding to the manager for 30 days after the
  last working day
Equipment returned to: the office manager, on the last working day
Device wipes and mail settings: IT lead
```

### 6. Notes (from the HR lead)

```
- The coordinator also approves supplier invoices in the supplier
  invoicing portal for Harbour Road. It is not on our access register.
- They hold keys to the plan room. Not on the equipment list.
- The coordinator would like to tell the team themselves at the stand-up
  on Monday 16 November.
```

- **From:** access register (7 rows); equipment list (4 items); handover list (4 items); offboarding standard; notes.
- **Lines:** 17 (4 handover, 8 access, 5 equipment). **Lines flagged:** 9. **Decisions:** 9.
- **Left out:** the reason for leaving, given in the departure record.

**Decisions before this checklist is shared: 9**

1. **H2, receiver not named.** For: Head of delivery. The monthly resource plan is next due on Monday 7 December, after the last working day, and the Finance manager relies on it. Who takes it over?
2. **H3, after the last day.** For: Head of delivery. The Harbour Road close-out is due on Friday 18 December, and the list hands it to the Senior consultant by Friday 11 December, a week after the last working day. By which date on or before Friday 4 December is it handed over?
3. **H4, kept in not stated.** For: Head of delivery. Where are the supplier invoice approvals for Harbour Road kept? The HR lead's notes say the coordinator approves these invoices in the supplier invoicing portal (A8).
4. **A3, admin rights.** For: IT lead. The coordinator has Admin access to the project tracker, which the Head of delivery owns. Is another admin in place before 17:30 on Friday 4 December?
5. **A5, no admin owner.** For: IT lead. The client file-sharing site uses a shared delivery team login, and the register names nobody to change its sign-in when the coordinator leaves. Who owns it?
6. **A6, data receiver not named.** For: Head of delivery. The video meetings account holds recordings. The handover list names the Project coordinator for the mailbox and files only. Who receives the recordings?
7. **A8, not on the register.** For: IT lead. The HR lead's notes say the coordinator approves supplier invoices for Harbour Road in the supplier invoicing portal, which is not on the access register. The register gives no account, access level or admin owner for it. Who removes this access, and does the portal join the register?
8. **E1, return method to set.** For: HR lead. The laptop is kept at home. Does the coordinator bring it in on Friday 4 December, or is it collected?
9. **E5, not on the equipment list.** For: HR lead. The notes say the coordinator holds keys to the plan room; the equipment list does not include them and nothing says where they are kept. Where are they kept, how do they come back to the office manager by Friday 4 December, and do they join the list?

**Outside this checklist:** pay, benefits, leaving paperwork, references, the exit conversation and any agreement with the departing senior project coordinator stay with the HR lead's own process. The reason for leaving is not recorded here.

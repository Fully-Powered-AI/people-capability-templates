# Site safety induction pack: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the depot, the contractor, the agency, the people, the documents and every date below are invented. The company is an invented chilled-food distributor, called Company D here, with a depot of forty people. Three new starters begin on Monday 16 November 2026, and two refrigeration engineers from a contractor arrive on Wednesday 18 November to service the cold store. The depot operations manager runs the capability; the health and safety coordinator approves the pack and delivers the induction. Organisations are referred to by letter.

First come the inputs the depot operations manager supplied, in full, then the pack the capability returned.

## What the capability returned

**SITE SAFETY INDUCTION PACK: Depot 2**

- Areas covered: warehouse floor, cold store, loading dock, battery charging bay, offices and canteen; the plant room for the contractor group only. Groups: New starters (Warehouse operative, Transport clerk); Contractors (Refrigeration engineer).
- Prepared by: depot operations manager on 10 November 2026. Approved and delivered by: health and safety coordinator. Delivery: in person, in the training room.
- Sources: D1 Emergency plan, revision 4, 1 September 2026; D2 First aid and welfare notice, 20 August 2026; D3 Depot 2 site rules, 1 September 2026; D4 Staff handbook, section 6, Reporting, 1 March 2026. Hazard list: 20 rows, 6 hazards. People list: 5 people.
- Settings: defaults used for: pass rule; hazard source age (12 months).
- Personal details: names, kept to Part 4; one health detail, left out.
- Records kept by: site administrator.

**PART 1: INDUCTION CONTENT**

**1. Emergencies**

- Fire alarm: a continuous electronic bell. Leave by the nearest green fire exit sign; do not go back for belongings; do not leave through the dock doors unless a fire marshal directs you (D1).
- Refrigerant alarm: a rising and falling siren with amber flashing beacons in the cold store and plant room. Leave the building by the nearest fire exit away from the plant room (D1).
- Assembly point: assembly point B, the far corner of the staff car park by the main gate (D1).
- Roll: the shift supervisor takes the roll at assembly point B from the sign-in list, which includes contractors (D1).
- Finding a fire: break the nearest red call point, leave, and tell the shift supervisor at the assembly point where the fire is. Red call points are at every exit (D1).
- Finding someone hurt: call a first aider on radio channel 1 and stay with the person if it is safe (D1).

**2. First aid and welfare**

- First aiders: on the board in the canteen corridor, and on radio channel 1 (D2).
- First aid room: next to the dock office. Eye wash station: on the wall at the battery charging bay (D2).
- Toilets, lockers and break area: by the main entrance. The canteen has drinking water, with a warm room next to it (D2).
- Feeling unwell: tell your shift supervisor. If you are very cold after the cold store, use the warm room (D2).

**3. Site rules**

- Signing in and out: everyone signs in and out on the reception screen (D3).
- Closed areas: no entry to the plant room or the roof without the maintenance supervisor's permission (D3).
- Protective equipment: a high-visibility vest beyond the office door; safety footwear on the warehouse floor, the loading dock, in the cold store and at the battery charging bay (D3).
- Walking routes: walk only in the green walkways; cross only at the gated crossings (D3).
- Vehicle routes: MISSING: vehicle routes. D3 says where people walk but not where forklifts run; see point 1.
- Phones: not while walking on the warehouse floor or the dock; use them in the canteen or the offices (D3).
- Smoking and vaping: only in the shelter by the main gate (D3).

**4. Site hazards and controls**

- **H1. Forklift striking a person.** Area: warehouse floor and loading dock. Applies to: All.
  - Engineering: barriered walkways with gated crossing points.
  - Rules and procedures: forklifts give way at gated crossings and keep to walking pace in the aisles.
  - Rules and procedures: pedestrians stay inside the green walkways and cross only at gated crossings.
  - Protective equipment: high-visibility vest beyond the office door.
  - Source: RA-04 Vehicles and pedestrians, 15 June 2026.
- **H2. Being shut in the cold store at -22 °C.** Area: cold store. Applies to: Warehouse operative; Refrigeration engineer.
  - Engineering: inside door release and lit alarm button beside every cold store door.
  - Rules and procedures: sign the cold store board in and out and carry a radio inside.
  - Rules and procedures: no more than 45 minutes inside at a time, then a 15-minute warm break.
  - Protective equipment: insulated jacket and gloves from the rack at the cold store door.
  - Source: RA-07 Cold store, 15 June 2026.
- **H3. Trailer pulling away from the dock during loading.** Area: loading dock. Applies to: Warehouse operative.
  - Engineering: dock lock holds the trailer, and the dock light turns green only when it is locked.
  - Rules and procedures: drivers hand their keys to the dock office before loading starts.
  - Rules and procedures: enter a trailer only when the dock light is green.
  - Source: RA-05 Loading dock, 15 June 2026.
- **H4. Gas and acid at the battery charging bay.** Area: battery charging bay. Applies to: Warehouse operative.
  - Engineering: extraction fan interlocked with the chargers.
  - Rules and procedures: only staff trained by the maintenance supervisor change or top up batteries.
  - Rules and procedures: no smoking or naked flames inside the yellow lines.
  - Protective equipment: face shield and acid-resistant gloves for topping up.
  - Source: RA-09 Battery charging, 10 February 2025.
- **H5. Slipping on ice by the cold store doors.** Area: floor outside the cold store doors. Applies to: Warehouse operative; Refrigeration engineer.
  - Rules and procedures: report ice to the shift supervisor at once, and it is cleared and coned off.
  - Protective equipment: slip-resistant safety footwear.
  - Source: RA-07 Cold store, 15 June 2026.
  - Only rules and protective equipment recorded: see point 3.
- **H6. Refrigerant leak in the plant room.** Area: plant room. Applies to: Refrigeration engineer.
  - Engineering: leak detectors start the plant room extraction and sound the refrigerant alarm.
  - Rules and procedures: enter the plant room only with a permit issued by the maintenance supervisor.
  - Protective equipment: carry the personal gas monitor issued with the permit.
  - Source: RA-11 Plant room, 15 June 2026.
- Not in this pack: none.

**PART 3: CHECK OF UNDERSTANDING**

- **Q1** (Emergencies; All). What does a rising and falling siren with amber flashing beacons mean, and which way do you leave?
  Answer: the refrigerant alarm. Leave the building by the nearest fire exit away from the plant room (D1).
- **Q2** (First aid and welfare; All). How do you reach a first aider, and where is the first aid room?
  Answer: on radio channel 1, or from the board in the canteen corridor. The first aid room is next to the dock office (D2).
- **Q3** (Site rules; All). Beyond which door must you wear a high-visibility vest?
  Answer: beyond the office door (D3).
- **Q4** (Reporting; All). You see a near miss on the dock. Who do you tell, and when?
  Answer: your shift supervisor, straight away; contractors also tell the maintenance supervisor. The supervisor writes a near-miss card at the dock office the same shift (D4).
- **Q5** (H1; All). Where may you walk on the warehouse floor and the dock, and where may you cross a forklift route?
  Answer: only inside the green walkways; cross only at the gated crossings (H1).
- **Q6** (H2; Warehouse operative; Refrigeration engineer). Before you go into the cold store, what do you sign and what do you carry?
  Answer: sign the cold store board in and out, and carry a radio inside (H2).
- **Q7** (H3; Warehouse operative). When may you enter a trailer at the dock?
  Answer: only when the dock light is green, which shows the dock lock is holding the trailer (H3).
- **Q8** (H4; Warehouse operative). Who may change or top up a battery at the charging bay?
  Answer: only staff trained by the maintenance supervisor, with a face shield and acid-resistant gloves for topping up (H4).
- **Q9** (H5; Warehouse operative; Refrigeration engineer). You find ice on the floor by a cold store door. What do you do?
  Answer: report it to the shift supervisor at once; it is cleared and coned off (H5).
- **Q10** (H6; Refrigeration engineer). What must you have before you enter the plant room?
  Answer: a permit issued by the maintenance supervisor, and the personal gas monitor issued with it (H6).
- **Q11** (Permits; Refrigeration engineer). Which work at Depot 2 needs a permit, and when does the permit go back?
  Answer: work at height, hot work and any work on the refrigeration plant, with a permit from the maintenance supervisor before the work starts. It goes back to the maintenance supervisor at the end of each day (D3).

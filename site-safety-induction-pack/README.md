# Site safety induction pack

For one named site, the site, facilities or people lead gets an induction built from that site's own emergency arrangements, rules and hazards, with a short check of understanding, a sign-off record for each person and refresher dates by role. A competent person approves it and delivers it.

*Workflow template · People and HR · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/site-safety-induction-pack/)*

## The outcome

Every new starter and every visiting contractor hears about your site, not a site in general. They learn the sound of your alarms, the name of your assembly point, the walkways your forklifts give way at and the door release inside your cold room, each line traced to the plan, the rules or the risk assessment it came from. Where your documents leave something out, the pack says so plainly instead of papering over it with stock wording.

The hazards come from your own risk assessments, matched to the roles that meet them, with the strongest controls first. A hazard held back only by a sign and a pair of boots is pointed out to your competent person, who decides whether that is enough. Nothing is added that your site has not recorded.

Each person then shows they understood: a few questions about your site, a walk to the places that matter in an emergency, and for riskier roles a short demonstration. Each person's entry has a space for the result of every part of that check, so a signature alone never stands in for understanding, and the refresher dates are already set. Your competent person approves the pack and runs the induction. The AI assistant signs nobody off and lets nobody onto the site.

## Who starts the work, and when

The site manager, facilities manager or HR administrator responsible for the people starting runs it a few days before they arrive: new hires, temporary and agency workers, or a contractor's crew coming in for a job. It runs again when the emergency plan, the rules or a hazard changes, and when refreshers fall due.

The person your company names as competent to induct people at that site, such as its health and safety coordinator, approves the pack and delivers the induction.

## What they supply

- **A short brief:** the site and its areas, who is starting and in which roles, the contractor's work and where its safety prequalification stands, who approves and delivers, and who keeps the records.
- **The site's own documents,** each with its date: the emergency plan, first aid and welfare, the site rules, the permit arrangements and how to report a problem. Typed summaries are fine.
- **The hazard list** from your risk assessments, one control per row, in seven stated columns.
- **The list of people** being inducted, in five stated columns.
- **Your settings:** how each role is checked, any demonstration tasks, and how often each role is refreshed. Sensible defaults are supplied.

No connection to a training system, a door-entry system or any register is needed, and none is asked for.

## What comes out

One pack in a fixed order:

1. **The induction content:** emergencies, first aid and welfare, site rules, the site's hazards with their controls, and reporting, each line citing its document.
2. **A part for contractors:** the permits, who issues them, the rules that apply to contractors only, and their prequalification as you gave it.
3. **The check of understanding:** the questions with their answer key, the walk-round places for each role, any demonstrations and the pass rule.
4. **The sign-off record:** one entry per person, the only place names appear, with the results and signatures left blank for the induction.
5. **Refresher dates** by role, with what brings one forward and who owns them.
6. **Points for your competent person,** such as a missing arrangement or an out-of-date risk assessment, and a blank approval block.

Health details never appear in the pack. If one turns up in the notes, the assistant leaves it out, names only the kind of detail in the pack's header, and after the pack tells you to take it up directly with the people team and your competent person.

## What has to change for your company

- **Who approves and delivers.** Name your competent person for each site, and who stands in.
- **How each role is checked.** Questions only, questions and a walk-round, or both with a demonstration, and which tasks are demonstrated.
- **Your pass rule,** if it differs from the default.
- **Refresher timing** for each role, and after how long away someone needs one again.
- **How old a risk assessment may be** before the pack questions it.
- **Where signed records are kept,** and who owns the refresher dates.
- **Your adviser.** The pack cites no law or standard; whoever advises you confirms what your country and sector require.
- **Your platform.** It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, from the documents you supply.

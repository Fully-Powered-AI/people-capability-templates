# Culture health check: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the people, the client, the survey and every figure below are invented. Harrowfinch Studio is an invented 64-person design and web agency with four teams. Its people lead runs a culture health check after the October staff survey, for the studio's two founders. First come the inputs the people lead supplied, in full, then the check the workflow template returned.

## The inputs

### 1. Settings

```

Headcount: 64, in four teams: Design, Engineering, Client Services, Operations
Today: Tuesday 10 November 2026
Period: October 2026 survey; exit notes and decisions from April to
  October 2026; recognition from July to September 2026
Run by: People lead
Leadership: the two founders
Rituals already in place: monthly all-hands with a founders' update; an
  onboarding buddy for each new starter; a weekly meeting in each team;

### 6. Exit notes, April to October 2026 (typed by the people lead)

```
1. Engineering, left July. Priorities changed after the June team change
   and nobody explained why. Valued having work reviewed before clients
   saw it.
2. Client Services, left May. Felt that raising client problems with
   their lead got them seen as negative. Wanted a clearer path to
   promotion.
3. Design, left June. Moving to another city. Praised the studio's
   quality bar.
4. Engineering, left August. Support tickets bounced between teams with
   nobody owning them. Heard about the June team change from a client
   before hearing it from the founders.

   by their lead, Wren Kafton, to "keep it positive". Said pay was below

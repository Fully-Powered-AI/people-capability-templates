# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Three files of the culture-architect package in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT) shaped the method. From the package's main instructions in c-level-advisor/skills/culture-architect: reading culture from what a company recognises and puts up with rather than from what it says, writing each value as behaviours a newcomer could notice, the areas a culture survey covers (raising concerns, clarity, fairness, growth and trust in leadership), anti-patterns such as values nobody practises, culture left to spread unaided and habits kept from a much smaller company, the warning signs of survey results never shared with the team and standards applied unevenly, and choosing rituals to suit the company's size. From c-level-advisor/skills/culture-architect/references/culture-playbook.md: grounding each value in real examples and a counter-example, putting the largest fall since the last survey first rather than folding it into an average, checking what gets recognised against the stated values, and ritual ideas such as a weekly end-of-week round of what went right and what to do differently, a month-in conversation for new starters, a survey read-back to staff, a rotating cross-team group and an open hour with the founders. From c-level-advisor/skills/culture-architect/templates/culture-code-template.md: pairing each value with what it does not look like, and treating how decisions are really made as a place culture shows. Its workshop for writing a mission and vision, its culture code document, its score bands and its use of culture in performance reviews and hiring are not used. Fully Powered AI built the numbered evidence and the rules for reading each behaviour as matching, mixed, drifting or short of evidence, the thresholds a company sets, the smallest group reported and the merging of small teams, exit themes reported for the whole company only and counted only when two leavers raise them, the two-sign rule before an anti-pattern is named, the survey export headers with a stop on any mismatch, the wording of every ritual, the removal of names, and the rule that leadership decides what changes and nothing is shared with staff by the assistant. No third-party text is reused.

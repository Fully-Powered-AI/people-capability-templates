# Culture health check

A founder or people lead sees whether the company's stated values show up in daily work: each value written as behaviours you could watch for, read against the team's own evidence, with where practice matches, where it drifts, and two or three rituals to try. Reported by team and theme, never about a person.

*Workflow template · People and HR · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/culture-health-check/)*

## The outcome

Leadership stops guessing whether the values on the website still describe the company. Each value becomes two or three things a new starter could actually notice in an ordinary week, and each of those is set against what the company already knows: the staff survey, what leavers said on the way out, how the last few big decisions were really made, and what people get thanked for. Every behaviour gets a plain reading (matches, mixed, drifts, or not enough evidence) with the numbered evidence behind it, so nobody has to take the verdict on trust.

The check names the patterns that quietly wear a culture down, such as decisions that reach a team late and without reasons, but only where two separate pieces of evidence point the same way. It then proposes two or three rituals sized for the company, each tied to the drift it answers, and lists the questions only leadership can settle.

It is about the company, never about a person. Results are shown by team and theme; small teams are merged so nobody's answer can be picked out; names are removed from every note. Leadership decides what changes and what staff hear.

## Who starts the work, and when

The founder, the people lead or the head of operations runs it after a staff survey closes, before an annual look at the values, after a merger or a spell of fast hiring, or when exit conversations keep circling the same complaint. It runs again after the next survey to see what moved. The founders or the leadership team read it, confirm or reword the behaviours, and choose what to try.

## What they supply

- **The stated values,** with the description the company uses for each, and any behaviours already written down.
- **The staff survey export,** counts by team and question, with the question wording, and last time's results if there are any.
- **Exit notes,** one per leaver, ideally with names already taken out.
- **Notes on recent decisions** that changed how people work: who proposed, who was asked, who decided and how it was told.
- **What people were recognised for,** as counts by theme.
- **A few settings:** headcount, the rituals already in place, the smallest group the company is willing to report, and how high a survey score has to be to count as a match.

Any one source of evidence is enough to start; more sources make the readings firmer.

## What comes out

One culture health check, ready for leadership:

- the evidence, numbered, so every reading can be traced;
- each value written as behaviours, with drafted ones marked for leadership to confirm;
- where practice matches and where it drifts, behaviour by behaviour, with any team that sits well below the company;
- a view by team with small teams merged, and what changed since the last survey, the largest fall first;
- the anti-patterns the evidence shows, the ones with only one sign, and the ones not seen;
- two or three rituals to try, each with what it answers, who runs it and what to watch next time;
- the questions for leadership, and what was left out and why.

Nothing is shared with staff, sent or posted.

## What has to change for your company

Set the smallest group you will ever report; five is a careful floor and small, close-knit teams may want more. Decide the survey scores that count as a match and as a drift, and the gap between a team and the company worth showing. Map your survey questions to your values once so every run reads them the same way. Supply your own behaviours once leadership has confirmed them. Trim the ritual menu to what would suit your company and add rituals you already trust. Name who sees the check and who decides what staff are told. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini from the files you supply; it needs no connection to your survey tool or HR system.

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets.

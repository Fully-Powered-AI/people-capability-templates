Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. From the policy lookup method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes working from handbook text the person pastes in; a short direct answer ahead of the detail; the exact policy wording quoted beside a plain explanation; noting the exceptions the policy states; citing the document and section; saying so when no policy is found instead of filling the gap; naming who to ask about cases the text does not settle; and sending legal or compliance questions to HR. Fully Powered AI wrote the version that reads a question export with stated column headers, gives every question one of five statuses, checks whether the quoted text covers the asker's employee group, shows which headings were checked when the handbook is silent, quotes both sides when two documents disagree, sends every question about one person's own entitlement or circumstances to the HR contact with no conclusion about that person, drafts a reply the person reviews and sends, and gives no view on the law. No third-party text is reused.

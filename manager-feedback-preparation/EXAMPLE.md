# Manager feedback preparation: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented 120-person freight and warehousing company.

**Feedback preparation · Warehouse team lead · Thursday 12 November 2026**

- **Prepared for:** The warehouse shift manager.
- **Responsibility in focus:** Check every outbound load against the load plan before release.
- **Start with:** 27 October: the 6 a.m. briefing covered the three priority loads, the dock-door change and break cover. Every crew member asked afterwards knew their first task. Source: the shift manager sat in.
- **Observation:** 3 November: Load 7718 was released without the second check. Two pallets for another customer arrived at the customer’s dock. Source: release log and the customer’s return note.
- **Interpretation to test:** The shift manager’s note: “Rushed because the truck was late.” Ask; do not state.
- **Questions:** What did you know when Load 7718 was ready? What made the second check hard that morning? What would let a late truck wait for the check?
- **The ask:** Every outbound load gets the second check before release, late truck or not. If the check cannot happen, the load waits and the shift manager is called.
- **Support on offer:** A second checker on the early dock when two or more trucks are late.
- **Last time, 6 October:** Shift manager to repair the door 4 scanner: done on 14 October. Team lead to train two crew members on the check: one done on 20 October, one open.
- **Sent back:** “Could take more ownership of the end-of-shift tidy.” No date and no example. Which shift, and what was left undone?

Preparation for the shift manager. The conversation and the development plan agreed in it belong to the shift manager and the team lead.

# Employee onboarding plan: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented 120-person freight and warehousing company.

**Onboarding plan · Customer operations coordinator · Starts Monday 16 November 2026**

- **Prepared for:** The customer operations manager, as hiring manager.
- **Before day one:** Confirm with IT that order-system and carrier-portal access is requested. Confirm the buddy, a senior coordinator. Write and send the welcome note.
- **Week one:** Monday: people lead welcome, site tour, safety induction. Tuesday: read the shipment-exception procedure; shadow the buddy on status calls. Wednesday: order-system guide on the training account. Thursday: meet the transport desk lead and a warehouse shift manager. Friday: answer five routine inquiries with the buddy reviewing each; first check-in.
- **Day 30:** Answers routine shipment-status inquiries alone. The buddy reviews five each week.
- **Day 60:** Handles carrier delay exceptions to the agreed procedure. The manager reads each customer message before it goes.
- **Day 90:** Owns day-to-day inquiries for 20 accounts and writes the night-desk handover note on their shifts.
- **Check-ins:** Weekly for four weeks, then at days 30, 60 and 90: what went well, what got stuck, the next milestone, the support needed.
- **For the manager:** Responsibility 5, “Raise damage claims with carriers”, has no training material. Name who teaches it and when. The order-system guide is dated March 2025, past the team’s 12-month limit. Confirm it matches the current screens before Wednesday.

A draft for the hiring manager, who adapts it and judges progress at each check-in. The new colleague adds to it in week one.

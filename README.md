# Fully Powered Capability Templates: People and HR

Every manager as prepared as your best one. These capability templates take the work of bringing people in and helping them grow, and give an AI assistant a precise way to prepare it. The manager and the people team make every decision about a person.

This repository holds 11 capability templates for people teams and the managers they support. Each one is a single piece of work with its own folder: what it is for and who starts it, the complete template an AI assistant follows, a worked example with invented data, what to change for your company, what has been checked, and its sources.

## The capabilities

- **[Culture health check](culture-health-check/)**: A founder or people lead sees whether the company's stated values show up in daily work: each value written as behaviours you could watch for, read against the team's own evidence, with where practice matches, where it drifts, and two or three rituals to try. Reported by team and theme, never about a person.
- **[Departing-employee knowledge capture](departing-employee-knowledge-capture/)**: When someone is leaving or changing role, their manager gets an interview guide aimed at what only that person knows, then a handover document the successor can work from, every point traced to the conversations and marked with how sure it is. Nothing is shared until the manager approves.
- **[Employee health plan comparison](employee-health-plan-comparison/)**: For an owner or people lead at renewal: the broker's health plan options next to each other, with premiums split between company and employee, deductibles, out-of-pocket limits, network notes, the cost to the company and to a typical employee at low, middle and high use, and questions for the broker.
- **[Employee offboarding and handover checklist](employee-offboarding-and-handover-checklist/)**: When someone leaves, your HR lead and their manager share one checklist: every piece of their work handed to a named colleague before the last day, every system on your access register removed by its owner at a set time, and every item of equipment back. Nothing is removed or sent without a person.
- **[Employee onboarding plan](employee-onboarding-plan/)**: Every new colleague starts with a first week and a first 90 days planned to the standard of your best manager: what to read, who to meet, what to practice and what productive means at day 30, 60 and 90.
- **[Headcount plan](headcount-plan/)**: For a founder, people lead or finance lead preparing next year: every role and its cost by team, the new roles and backfills with their months, the fully loaded cost month by month against the budget, and what changes if revenue lands lower or higher. It plans roles and money, never decisions about named people.
- **[Internal change announcement](internal-change-announcement/)**: One decided change, announced well: managers briefed first, a message for each group in the right order, talking points, and the questions people will ask, answered only from the decision or marked open. Every message is a draft that a person approves and sends.
- **[Manager feedback preparation](manager-feedback-preparation/)**: Every manager walks into a feedback conversation prepared like your best one: specific dated observations, the questions worth asking, a clear ask and the support on offer.
- **[Policy answers from the handbook](policy-answers-from-the-handbook/)**: Staff questions about leave, hours, expenses and conduct get answers that quote your own handbook, name the section, and say plainly where the handbook is silent. Anything about one person's own entitlement goes to HR, and no reply leaves until a person has read it.
- **[Site safety induction pack](site-safety-induction-pack/)**: For one named site, the site, facilities or people lead gets an induction built from that site's own emergency arrangements, rules and hazards, with a short check of understanding, a sign-off record for each person and refresher dates by role. A competent person approves it and delivers it.
- **[Training needs analysis](training-needs-analysis/)**: For one team, the HR lead or team manager gets what each role must be able to do, how many people can do it now, where the team falls short, and the training to arrange first, with who arranges it and by when. Counted by role, never a rating of a named person.

## How to use one

Open a folder and start with its README. Every folder stands alone, so copy the one you want and leave the rest. The templates are written for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot and Gemini: paste the instruction block and the output format in as custom instructions, or load the folder in a product that reads `SKILL.md` packages.

In every template a person starts the work, supplies the inputs and decides. The template prepares and checks. Anything that sends, posts, pays or changes a record waits for a person to approve it.

## What SKILL.md is

Each folder holds a file named `SKILL.md`. It is the technical file: the same instructions and output format as the template, in the packaged form that AI products which load instructions from a folder read. It is generated from `TEMPLATE.md`, so the two always agree. The capability template is the whole folder; `SKILL.md` is one way of loading it. If your AI product does not read that format, use `TEMPLATE.md`.

## What "checked" means here

Each folder says exactly what was checked and when: who wrote it, that its sources and rights were reviewed, the date of its editorial review, and whether it has been tried on its invented example in a named AI product. Nothing is described as tested for a client unless it was. Test a template on your own past work before your team relies on it; each one comes with acceptance tests for that.
